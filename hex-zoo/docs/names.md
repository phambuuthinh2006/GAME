# Working title and name candidates

The build uses **Hex Zoo** as an explicitly temporary title. These are creative proposals only; availability, trademarks and store collisions have **not** been checked. The owner will check the App Store, Google Play and CrazyGames before choosing.

1. **Pawpatch Parade** — Warm and easy to pronounce; suggests animals joining a growing board. Check both joined and spaced spellings.
2. **Wildwoven** — A compact invented word about connecting animal families. Check books, apps and studios as well as game titles.
3. **Habitat Huddle** — Explains the adjacency idea without promising a simulation game. Both words are common, so collision checks matter.
4. **Critter Cartography** — Distinctive map-making feel; longer at small thumbnail sizes. Check whether younger players understand it.
5. **Pawprint Mosaic** — Emphasizes an animal placement puzzle and allows a strong visual identity. Check singular/plural variants.

None of these names should be placed on final covers until the owner chooses and clears it. Final image prompts contain no text; title lettering is a separate asset/overlay so a title change does not require repainting.
